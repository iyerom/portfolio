about me

I'm a year old 2nd year Electrical Engineering student at UBC Okanagan, and I like making things.
Right now, I'm actively rebuilding my Hexapod project, and also working on an ESP32 smartwatch.

I'm particularly interested in robotics, embedded systems, and automation. Most of what I build starts as a random idea inspired by something on the internet.

Outside of engineering, I dabble in a bunch of programming projects. I made this website from barebones javascript, html, and css as a fun learning experience, and also program various simulations in a variety of languages. I also like to make small games in my free time, and join a few game jams every now and then.

When I'm not in front of a screen, I like to
go indoor bouldering (hit me up if you'd be down to go),
carving little wood trinkets (and sometimes bigger stuff),
and playing the piano (although not very well - yet.)

I also work as a Math and Physics tutor, which opens me up to a lot of different ways of thinking and problem solving, while also honing the skill of verbalizing my thoughts.

That's a little about me. If any of this interests you, feel free to reach out to me on my socials!
